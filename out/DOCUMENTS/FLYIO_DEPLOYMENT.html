### How Fly.io Works

Internet (Port 80/443) ↓ Fly.io Edge Proxy (handles SSL, routing) ↓ Your Container (Port 5000) fly.toml configuration: toml[http_service] internal_port = 5000 # ← Your app listens here force_https = true

Fly.io maps these external ports to your internal_port

Port 80 → redirects to 443

Port 443 → proxies to your internal_port (5000)

Why NOT Bind to Port 80? ❌ Problems with binding to port 80:

Requires root - Ports < 1024 need root privileges No SSL - You'd have to handle certificates yourself Platform doesn't expect it - Fly.io, Railway, Render all expect high ports

✅ Advantages of port 5000:

No root needed - Runs as non-root user (more secure) Platform handles SSL - Free HTTPS automatic Standard practice - All PaaS platforms work this way
